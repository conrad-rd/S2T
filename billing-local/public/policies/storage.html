Website

The appearance demo runs in your browser. Its adjustments are held in page memory. The policy pages contain no scripts and make no analytics calls. Fonts and app artwork are served with the site.

Sign-in and payments

The credits dashboard loads Clerk for sign-in. Clerk uses cookies and browser storage needed for sessions and security. Optional Clerk SDK telemetry is disabled. Signing in may load Cloudflare bot protection. Stripe Checkout opens only when you start a purchase and can use payment and fraud-prevention storage under Stripe’s policy. These third parties receive connection information when their services load.

Local preview

The development preview uses an HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict s2t_session cookie with a seven-day maximum age. It is not the authentication mechanism for the hosted Clerk account.

App storage

The Mac app stores preferences locally and credentials in Keychain. Optional clipboard history is encrypted locally and bounded to 50 entries and 48 hours. Dictionary entries, instructions, models and selected reference images are local files. See the privacy policy for when their contents may be used in a cloud request.

Control

You can clear site storage or block cookies through your browser. Doing so may sign you out or prevent authentication and payment protection from working. S2T does not install optional advertising or analytics cookies that need an accept-all banner. Adding such collection would require a separate choice before it runs.

Keep a copy using your browser’s Save or Print command.
