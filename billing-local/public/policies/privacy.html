Withdrawal submissions

The withdrawal form collects your name, contact email, purchase description and receipt delivery choice without requiring sign-in. S2T stores the declaration and its receipt timestamp encrypted, so the operator can process it and demonstrate when it arrived. A submission does not verify ownership or automatically refund money. The browser receives a downloadable text receipt. No automatic confirmation email is sent. These records are retained with contract and claims evidence, subject to the financial-record retention described below.

Who is responsible

Conrad Baulig operates S2T and is responsible for personal data processed to run the app and hosted credits service. Contact details are in the legal notice. This notice covers S2T, its website and credits dashboard.

Optional Contributor models

Muse Spark Contributor is available only after you explicitly allow Meta to use prompts and responses to improve its products. The checkbox is off by default and applies only to that model and task. Choosing a different model does not carry over that permission. You can withdraw it in Models before another request. Other OpenRouter requests keep the app’s data-collection-deny setting.

What leaves your Mac

Cloud transcription sends the recording you start to the selected speech provider. Text cleanup sends your transcript and editing instructions, including applicable dictionary entries, to the selected text provider. Hosted credits routes these requests through S2T. With your own provider key, the app contacts that provider directly. Local models run on your Mac; a custom endpoint or Codex CLI follows the service and settings you choose. Downloading models still requires a network connection.

Recording recovery on your Mac

Before uploading a finished recording, S2T saves an encrypted local recovery copy of its audio, transcription progress and available transcript. Its encryption key stays in macOS Keychain. Unfinished copies survive app restarts and can be opened from Last dictation → Saved recordings. After successful delivery or a valid empty result, S2T removes the recovery copy. Historical streaming receipt metadata remains encrypted for service confirmation. Its local presence does not mean a financial hold is still outstanding; contact S2T support to check reconciliation. A failed or cancelled request keeps its recovery copy until it is successfully delivered. Save recording exports an unencrypted WAV file to a location you choose. If local saving fails, S2T keeps the audio in memory and asks you to save it before quitting. When macOS requests sleep or a normal quit, S2T attempts to stop capture and save the audio already recorded. A forced termination, crash or power loss can still interrupt that save.

Optional context

Clipboard history is off by default for new installations. If you enable it, S2T remembers up to 50 text copies for up to 48 hours in an encrypted local file, with its encryption key in Keychain. Saved choices remain in effect after an update. Clipboard reference matching runs locally. The editing model receives opaque placeholders instead of matched copied values, which S2T restores after editing. Text that you dictate yourself can still contain personal information. Disabling history stops new collection but keeps existing history. Use Clear clipboard history to clear it.

Accessibility and dictionary learning

Accessibility supports shortcuts, text delivery and locating the focused input. Input targeting reads roles, geometry and the nonsecure destination field text to keep an in-memory content fingerprint. If that text changes before delivery, S2T retains the result for manual insertion instead of restoring an outdated selection. Destination tracking does not save or upload the field text. After successful delivery, dictionary learning may briefly read the receiving nonsecure field to identify stable one- or two-word corrections. Observation stops on focus changes and lasts at most one minute. Learned replacements are saved in your local dictionary, which is included in later cleanup instructions.

Prompt mode and images

Prompt mode is optional and requires Screen Recording permission. During a prompt recording it samples full displays into a bounded memory history so spoken references can resolve to an image. Manually selected captures and images matched to your references can be sent to your selected image provider with your words. Selected reference images are also saved locally for attachment and recovery. The full display history is not uploaded. Saved reference files are not automatically erased after delivery. Other people or private content visible in a selected image may be included, so choose references carefully.

Accounts, credits and payments

Clerk handles sign-in and the identity information you provide to it, such as your email address. S2T stores an account identifier derived from your sign-in identity. During the restricted beta, verified email is checked against the access allowlist. Stripe handles payment details. The S2T ledger stores payment references, amounts, timestamps, credit debits and refunds, provider/model identifiers, request status and a request fingerprint to prevent duplicate charges. It does not store card numbers or raw input recordings. App keys are stored as hashes with expiry and revocation metadata.

Prepaid guest keys

Guest purchases do not require a sign-in account. One prepaid key is issued after confirmed payment. A necessary secure browser cookie remembers access for 180 days. A separate recovery code retrieves the same key; S2T stores only its hash. Guest keys can be reproduced securely by the service for authorized recovery and have no scheduled expiry. If you opt into email recovery, S2T stores your confirmed checkout email encrypted and a keyed lookup value. Resend delivers recovery emails containing a single-use link that expires after 15 minutes, not your app key. Without the browser session, recovery code or opted-in email access, S2T cannot automatically restore the key. Guest purchase and recovery records have no automatic archival expiry; contact the operator to disable email recovery or request closure.

Device and security data

Hosted usage includes a random installation identifier and a generic label such as MacBook Pro for the last-used device display. The hosted service stores device attribution with request records. The dashboard uses it to show the last-used device. A retention change to keep only pending requests and the latest completed usage is prepared but has not yet been deployed. S2T does not request your personal computer name, serial number, contacts or precise location. Network services necessarily receive IP addresses and connection information. The hosted service uses IP addresses and account or key identifiers for rate limits. A change to use rotating keyed identifiers is prepared but has not yet been deployed.

How long data stays

Hosted result text is encrypted for retry recovery and becomes unavailable after one hour. Expiry prevents retrieval, but expired encrypted results and other transient records can remain in the hosted database. Automatic removal is prepared but has not yet been deployed. Connection codes expire after ten minutes. Local preview sessions expire after seven days; hosted sign-in follows Clerk session settings. Rate windows last one minute, or one hour for local session creation. Hosting recovery copies can outlive active-database cleanup and follow the hosting provider’s retention settings.

Local files and financial records

API keys stay in macOS Keychain. Settings, prompts, dictionary entries, downloaded models and saved reference images remain on your Mac until changed or removed. The current dictation is held in app memory, and successfully delivered text is copied to your clipboard. Recent dictation text is saved in an encrypted local history, with its key in macOS Keychain, for up to 30 days, 500 entries or 8 MB, whichever limit is reached first. Delete individual entries or use Clear all in transcript history; Clear all also removes preserved unreadable history archives. Older plaintext history is migrated to encrypted storage before its plaintext source is removed. Unreadable older archives are preserved encrypted for manual recovery and remain until Clear all or removal. Matched clipboard values and recognized API-key formats are redacted from recent transcript history. Clearing clipboard history also removes identified clipboard-derived transcript entries; it does not alter an encrypted recording recovery copy needed to finish a pending request. The credits ledger retains accounting and audit records to maintain balances, reconcile provider charges and handle disputes. It currently has no automatic archival expiry. Contact us about deletion or closure; records needed for legal obligations or unresolved claims may have to be retained.

Purposes and legal grounds

We process requests, identity and credits to provide the service you request under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Necessary fraud prevention, authentication security and limited operational diagnostics rely on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f). Records legally required for accounting or responding to lawful requests rely on Article 6(1)(c). Optional access is controlled by feature settings and macOS permissions. Where consent is required, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Do not submit another person’s sensitive data without an appropriate basis to do so.

Service providers and international processing

Vercel serves the websites, Cloudflare runs the hosted credits API and database, Clerk provides authentication, and Stripe processes payments. Hosted AI requests use AssemblyAI or OpenRouter and its selected model host. Direct app choices can include Cerebras, Codex and your configured endpoints. These services may process information outside your country, including outside the EEA. Their applicable agreements and transfer safeguards govern that processing. Contact us for the safeguards and recipients relevant to your request. A local model does not make a separately selected cloud cleanup or image provider local.

No advertising or optional usage tracking

S2T does not add advertising pixels, session replay, cross-site tracking or optional product analytics. Clerk’s optional SDK telemetry is disabled. Worker observability logs are disabled in the supplied production configuration. Hosting, authentication, payment and AI providers can still process essential security and operational information. S2T does not sell your content or use it to train its own models. OpenRouter cleanup and image requests exclude providers marked as collecting data through the data_collection deny option, except for Muse Spark Contributor after its explicit model-specific opt-in. This is not a promise of zero retention across all providers, endpoints or account settings.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off optional context, choose local processing, revoke app keys, sign out and change macOS permissions. Email the operator to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, or to object to processing based on legitimate interests. We may need limited information to verify that the account is yours, but do not send passwords, API keys or recordings. GDPR requests normally receive a response within one month; lawful extensions will be explained. You may complain to the supervisory authority where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred. S2T does not make automated decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects.
