Report a problem

Email the operator with the affected feature, a description of the issue and safe reproduction steps using your own account or synthetic data. Do not send working credentials, other users’ content or recordings. Ask for a secure transfer method if sensitive evidence is necessary.

Responsible testing

Do not access another person’s account, disrupt service, make real charges or retain exposed personal data. Stop if testing reveals someone else’s information. Reporting a problem does not require proving it by downloading that information. This policy is not permission to test systems owned by our providers.

How S2T handles data

The app stores saved credentials in Keychain. Hosted app keys are hashed, retry results are encrypted, access checks separate accounts, and credit changes are recorded in an immutable ledger. Optional analytics are disabled. These controls reduce risk but are not a security certification or a claim that a system cannot fail.

Response

We review reports, investigate their impact and contact the reporter when follow-up is needed. If a personal-data breach occurs, we assess notification duties and inform affected people and authorities where required. This is not a paid bug-bounty program.
