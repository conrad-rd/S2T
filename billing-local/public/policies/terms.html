Providers and results

Provider availability, models and prices can change. With your own keys or endpoints, you have a separate relationship with those providers and pay their charges directly. If cleanup fails after successful transcription, S2T can deliver the original transcript and show an error.

Prepaid credits

Hosted credits are one-time purchases in USD, not a subscription. New purchases give 500 credits for $5, 1,100 for $10, 2,300 for $20, 5,800 for $50 and 11,800 for $100. Custom amounts between $5 and $100 use linear interpolation between these prices, rounded down to the nearest $0.000001 of provider value. Each credit covers $0.005 of metered provider cost. The total purchase price includes the S2T service charge. Existing balances and the provider value quoted in checkouts already started are preserved. Exact consumption depends on the model and usage. S2T reserves a bounded amount before submitting a request, then settles the charge and releases unused reserved credits. An uncertain provider outcome can keep a hold pending reconciliation. Credits are account-bound service credit, not a bank balance or transferable currency. The current system does not schedule credit expiry.
