Contact

For a duplicate payment, missing credits, disputed usage, a defective service or withdrawal from a purchase, email the operator. Include your account email, purchase date and Stripe receipt or payment reference if available. Do not send full card details, passwords, API keys or recordings.

One-time payments

S2T credits are prepaid, one-time purchases. There is no recurring subscription to cancel. Cancelling Stripe Checkout before payment does not add credits. After payment, contact us to review a refund or withdrawal. An uncertain usage hold is separate from a completed debit and needs reconciliation.

Unused paid credits

You may request a refund of unused paid credit through the contact details above. We calculate unused value from the original payment, prior refunds and actual settled usage. Promotional or complimentary credits have no cash refund value. Starting to use credit does not make an entire $5 block nonrefundable. Pending or uncertain usage is reviewed to establish the correct amount and is not treated as completed usage. Any internal provider-funding batches do not reduce your refund rights. Charges for service already supplied remain subject to statutory withdrawal rights, billing corrections and remedies for defective services.

Consumer withdrawal rights

Consumers may withdraw from a credit purchase without giving a reason within 14 days after the contract is concluded. Notify Conrad Baulig at the postal address or email above with a clear statement before that deadline. You may use the optional form below. Starting the app or using credits does not waive this right. Any longer mandatory period or other statutory remedy remains available.

Reimbursement and used credits

For a valid statutory withdrawal, we reimburse the payment within 14 days after receiving notice, using the original payment method unless you agree otherwise, without a refund fee. S2T currently does not require a separate early-performance request and will not deduct usage from that statutory reimbursement. Outside statutory withdrawal, the unused-credit policy applies. Refunds use the original purchase price and credit grant, including volume bonuses.

Payment errors and disputes

Confirmed duplicate charges and credits not supplied will be investigated and corrected. A refund reverses the corresponding credit value so it cannot be spent again. A chargeback or insufficient balance after reversal may temporarily restrict the account while the payment is resolved. Your legal rights remain unchanged.
